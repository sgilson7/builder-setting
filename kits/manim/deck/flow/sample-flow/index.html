This animation in the Scene files deck

What it shows

A scene file is rendered once into a clip and a final frame per step, and the player shows them in the deck.

Every step

  1. Write the animation as a scene file: boxes, lines and steps, in whole pixels and milliseconds.
  2. Render it once, in light and dark: one clip and one final frame per step, and a manifest of what made them.
  3. The player shows each clip in turn, or only the final frames when the reader asks for less motion.
